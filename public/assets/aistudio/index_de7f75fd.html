About Rahmani International

Rahmani International is a technology-focused company dedicated to creating mobile applications, websites and digital products. The company focuses on combining useful functionality, modern interfaces and practical digital experiences.

Work spans the full product lifecycle — from designing and developing Android applications and responsive websites, through publishing on app stores and web hosting, to ongoing technical support and continuous improvement of released products.
