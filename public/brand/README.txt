RAHMANI INTERNATIONAL — LOGO MANAGEMENT GUIDE
=============================================

Replace or upload your official logo in GitHub at:

  public/brand/logo.png

Supported formats (configure in lib/site-config.ts if using another extension):
- PNG  (public/brand/logo.png)   [Default primary path]
- SVG  (public/brand/logo.svg)   [Included crisp fallback]
- WebP (public/brand/logo.webp)
- JPG  (public/brand/logo.jpg)

Workflow:
1. Upload or replace public/brand/logo.png in your GitHub repository.
2. Commit the change to your main branch.
3. Vercel automatically rebuilds and redeploys https://rahmaniinternational.in.
4. The updated logo appears automatically in the Header, Hero, About card,
   Footer, Mobile Navigation, and favicon.
