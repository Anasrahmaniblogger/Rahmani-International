The Android operating system, Google Play, or integrated service providers may process limited technical information to operate the App, maintain service reliability, prevent abuse, or diagnose errors. Depending on the implementation, this can include:

  • App version and installation or update information;
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and general configuration;
