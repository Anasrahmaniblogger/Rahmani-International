Important: Booyah Challenge hosts Free Fire, Free Fire India and BGMI community tournaments. Some tournaments may be free to join, and some may use in-app coins. Users are not required to invest or pay money to earn or participate in coin-based features as described in the app. Coins are app-based virtual points, not money, unless a specific feature clearly states otherwise.

1. About Booyah Challenge

Booyah Challenge is a tournament and gaming utility app operated by Rahmani International. It may provide tournament announcements, registration, match details, player or team information, results, and virtual coin-related features for supported games, including Free Fire, Free Fire India and BGMI.

The app is an independent community platform and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially endorsed by the publishers of these games unless expressly stated.

2. Information We May Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app or its connected services may handle information you choose to provide, such as:

  • In-game name, game UID, team details, and tournament registration information.
  • App account or profile details, if account features are available in your version.
  • Tournament participation, match results, and virtual coin balance or transaction history.
  • Messages or contact details you voluntarily send to our support team.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

We do not ask you to provide passwords for your game account. Never share your game password, OTP, or payment credentials with tournament staff.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Register and manage tournament entries, teams, lobbies, and match schedules.
  • Verify player details, publish tournament results, and maintain fair-play records.
  • Maintain virtual coin balances and prevent duplicate, fraudulent, or abusive activity.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important app or tournament updates.
  • Protect the app, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.
  • Meet applicable legal, safety, and platform requirements.

4. Free Tournaments, Coins & Rewards

Booyah Challenge may offer free tournaments and tournaments or features that use virtual coins. The app is designed to let users participate and earn coins without making a monetary investment, where the relevant tournament or feature is marked as free or coin-based.

  • Coins are virtual in-app units and have no cash value by default.
  • Coins cannot be treated as a guaranteed income, investment, or financial return.
  • Coin balances and rewards may be subject to tournament rules, verification, fair-play checks, and correction of errors.
  • Do not assume coins are withdrawable, transferable, or redeemable for cash unless the app explicitly provides and explains that feature.
  • We may investigate suspicious activity and adjust or remove improperly credited coins or results in accordance with app rules.

Participation is subject to the rules shown for each tournament. No tournament result or reward is guaranteed.

5. Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when needed to provide an app feature, operate a service you use, protect users and the platform, or comply with a valid legal requirement.

Booyah Challenge may rely on hosting, database, analytics, notification, or advertising providers depending on the version and features enabled. Those providers may process limited technical or app-use information under their own privacy terms. Please review the disclosures and consent choices presented in the app and on Google Play.

Public tournament information—such as a player name, team, UID, or result—may be displayed to other participants or users when necessary to run or publish a tournament. Avoid submitting information you do not want displayed.

6. Permissions & Device Information

The app may use internet access to load tournament content and online features. Any other permission, such as notifications or storage access, should be requested only when required for a feature and used for that stated purpose. You can manage app permissions in your Android settings.

7. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is kept only for as long as needed to operate tournaments, maintain records, resolve disputes, provide support, or meet legal and platform obligations. Retention periods may vary by information type and service provider.

8. Your Choices & Requests

You may contact us to ask about information associated with you, request correction, or request deletion where applicable. Some tournament records may need to be retained for integrity, dispute resolution, or legal reasons. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

9. Children & Age Requirements

Use Booyah Challenge only if you meet the age requirements shown on Google Play and applicable tournament rules. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.
